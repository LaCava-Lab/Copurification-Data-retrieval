Data Availability Statement The datasets presented in this study can be found in online repositories. The names of the repository/repositories and accession number(s) can be found in the article/ Supplementary Material . Ethics Statement Plasma samples from P. vivax-infected patients (PV) were collected at Tierralta, Colombia. The local ethical committee of Universidad de Córdoba reviewed and approved these studies (Acta #007 mayo 22 de 2017). Plasma from healthy donors (HD) was obtained at the Hospital Germans Trias i Pujol (Badalona, Barcelona, Spain) after expressed consent from the donors. Plasma from first-infected P. vivax patients from Barcelona was collected at the Hospital Clinic under the approval of the Hospital´s committee (identification number 4228). The patients/participants provided their written informed consent to participate in this study.
